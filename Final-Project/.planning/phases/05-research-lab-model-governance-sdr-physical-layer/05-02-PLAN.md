# Plan 05-02: Empirical BER/FER Curves, EW Statistics Battery, and SDR Flowgraph Verification

## Objective
Verify visual rendering of empirical BER vs SNR curves and 8-domain EW statistics battery in the Research Lab console, and validate physical-layer GNU Radio flowgraph configurations (`tx_lpi_v4.grc`, `rx_lpi_v4.grc`) operating at $245.76\text{ kS/s}$ and $2.484\text{ GHz}$.

<must_haves>

- Real-time I/Q constellation scatter plot and power spectral density (PSD) FFT plot visualize neural waveform properties.
- GNU Radio flowgraphs (`tx_lpi_v4.grc`, `rx_lpi_v4.grc`) confirm sample rate $245.76\text{ kS/s}$ and center frequency $2.484\text{ GHz}$.
- Full test suite `pytest tests/test_lpi_v4.py -v` passes with 100% success rate.
</must_haves>

<threat_model>
### T-04-01: Operator Confusion Over Active Transport Route and Failover Behavior
- **Threat:** Operators may fail to recognize whether a message is being routed over Motorola DMR, Simulated RF, or an offline transport, or assume auto-failover is active when strict pinning is enforced.
- **Impact:** Misrouted tactical packets, unexpected queue holds, and lack of situational awareness.
- **Mitigation:**
  - In `DevicesView.tsx`, display prominent active route pill: `ACTIVE ROUTE: [TRANSPORT_NAME]`.
  - Clearly label auto-failover switch: `AUTO-FAILOVER: ENABLED` vs `AUTO-FAILOVER: DISABLED (STRICT PINNING)`.
  - When active route is offline and failover is disabled, render `ROUTE_UNAVAILABLE (HOLDING IN QUEUE)`.

### T-04-02: Hidden Over-the-Air Wire Costs for DMR Payloads
- **Threat:** Operators composing messages without real-time packet sizing feedback might exceed single-burst radio limits unaware of the multi-burst channel airtime and latency implications.
- **Impact:** Channel congestion on narrowband tactical radio channels.
- **Mitigation:**
  - Live composer HUD in `StationConsoleColumn.tsx` dynamically calculates UTF-8 bytes, adds 28-byte GCM envelope, and displays `[DMR: N Bursts (XXX B Wire Budget)]`.

### T-04-03: Ambiguity Regarding STAR-V Tactical SDR Safety Boundary
- **Threat:** Operators or evaluators might assume STAR-V hardware is broken rather than intentionally and permanently locked out for regulatory and hardware safety.
- **Impact:** Attempts to bypass software lockout or confusion about radio readiness.
- **Mitigation:**
  - Interactive `StarVLockoutDossierModal.tsx` explicitly documents the 4 statutory prerequisites (ICD, Defense Auth, Station License, COMSEC Key) with red padlocks.
  - Interactive test bench provides live verification buttons proving the software boundary is active and recording audit events.
  - `MessageTelemetryDrawer.tsx` displays illuminated crimson lockout seal `[HARDWARE SAFETY LOCKOUT: STAR-V RF KEYING BLOCKED]`.

### T-04-04: Aerial Relay Avionics Misconfiguration and Silent Battery Depletion
- **Threat:** Operators unaware of relay battery or storage depletion could lose vital communication paths unexpectedly. Furthermore, uncertainty might exist over whether aerial relays could interfere with drone flight controls.
- **Impact:** Unplanned communications blackout and flight safety concerns.
- **Mitigation:**
  - Relay cards in `DevicesView.tsx` display real-time color-coded battery and storage gauges with explicit `AUTONOMOUS CUTOFF` badges when battery $\le 5\%$ or storage $\ge 98\%$.
  - Aerial relay card prominently displays `[FLIGHT SAFETY ISOLATION: ACTIVE]` verifying payload software has zero connection to flight avionics.
  - 3D `LinkTopologyView.tsx` visually marks cutoff relays with amber/red status and `[CUTOFF]` tags.
</threat_model>

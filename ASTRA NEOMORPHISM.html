Pipeline guide

What happens in a real CGAN workflow?

1
Condition + noise vector

Pass the requested modulation class and a latent vector to the generator.

2
Generator creates sample

The generator synthesizes a candidate IQ signal for that condition.

3
Discriminator evaluates

During training, real and synthetic examples help the networks improve.

4
Validate before use

Check class fidelity, spectral shape, SNR, and downstream classifier performance.
